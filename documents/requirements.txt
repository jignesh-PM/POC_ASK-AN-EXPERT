1. Purpose and Background
Morgan Advanced Materials wants a gated customer support portal where website visitors and existing customers (CPI end users) can, once verified, get help with documentation for all Thermal Ceramics product lines (datasheets, safety datasheets, installation manuals, certificates, brochures and technical papers), use technical tools, and ask Morgan's experts questions. This document explains what the portal must do, using plain language and user journeys so business and technical teams share the same picture before coding starts.
The requirements come from the â€œAsk the Expert â€“ Dummy Prototype v1â€ deck (March 2023), which sets out the landing page, the main content areas, the tools, the open versus restricted access model, and Morgan's expectations for the project.
Goals
Keep all content gated: users verify their email address with a one-time passcode (OTP) before seeing any documentation, which limits bot views and tells Morgan who is using the portal. Sign-up stays quick; profile details are asked for afterwards.
Give customers fast answers: an AI assistant answers first, and a real product-line expert is one click away.
Track who uses the portal and how, including during the free alpha period.
Let Morgan manage content, restricted tools and users without developer help.
Be GDPR-compliant and secure from day one.

How routing works
The customer picks a category from the home screen or the chat; if they donâ€™t, the AI suggests one from the question and the customer confirms it.
The AI answers first. When the customer clicks â€œTalk to an expertâ€, the chat goes to every Morgan User mapped to that category and they are notified; the first to accept owns the chat.
If no one accepts within the set time (e.g. 15 minutes during working hours), it goes to the backup, then to the General / Not sure coordinator.
If regional routing is switched on, chats go to the category team for the customerâ€™s region first (EMEA, Americas, Asia and Australia).
The Super Admin maps Morgan Users to categories in the admin panel. One person can cover several categories, and a category can have several people with one marked as product manager.
Morgan Users can transfer a chat to another category or person; the customer sees who is handling it.
5. User Journeys
Each journey describes what the user does, step by step, and what the portal does in response.
Journey 1 â€“ Visitor arrives at the gated portal
Visitor arrives at the portal homepage (e.g. from a Morgan email or website).
Chooses their region and language from the header (pre-selected from the browser and location; can be changed at any time). The whole portal, and all links to the Thermal Ceramics site, follow this choice.
Sees a public landing page that explains what the portal offers (product lines, 6 Main Areas, 4 Tools, Ask the Expert), with a padlock on every block. No documents, links or tools are visible, and gated pages are hidden from search engines.
Clicks any block (e.g. â€œProduct Informationâ€) and is asked to verify their email address with a one-time passcode to continue (see Journey 3).
Once verified, opens a product line (e.g. Fibres, Refractories, Microporous), sees its support documents, and can click a product family to go to the product page on the Thermal Ceramics official site (morganthermalceramics.com) for their selected region and language. The click is recorded as a â€œproduct viewedâ€ event.
Once verified, uses site search (e.g. â€œSuperwool blanket 1260Â°Câ€); search terms and results clicked are recorded against the customer's journey.
Journey 2 â€“ Alpha user logs in with a Morgan-issued ID
Morgan admin creates the user in the admin panel (full name, email, company, country, product interest).
The portal emails the user an invitation; the user enters their email on the portal and receives a one-time passcode (no password to remember).
User enters the code, accepts the privacy notice and terms, and is signed in. Profile details already entered by the admin are pre-filled.
From now on all page views, downloads, tool use and questions are recorded against this user.
Journey 3 â€“ Visitor or existing customer verifies with email + OTP
User clicks â€œSign in / Sign upâ€ or any locked block. New and returning users use the same screen.
Enters their email address (and, for new users, full name and GDPR consent tick boxes). An invisible bot check runs in the background.
Receives a 6-digit one-time passcode by email (valid for 10 minutes) and enters it. Too many wrong codes or requests temporarily block the email/IP.
Is signed in and returned to the content they wanted, now unlocked. The session stays active for 30 days on that device (configurable), after which a new OTP is needed.
Journey 4 â€“ Customer completes their profile
After the first sign-in, the customer sees a short â€œComplete your profileâ€ form (about 1 minute). They can skip it and are reminded later.
Answers the profile questions: region and country, company name, job role, industry, product lines / products they are interested in, and products they already use (multi-select from the official product lines).
Portal personalises the home page: their product lines' documents first, region and language pre-set, relevant tools highlighted.
Profile completion is needed before using restricted tools and talking to a human expert, so the expert has context (configurable by admin).
Answers are saved on the customer profile, shown to Morgan Users in the Customer panel, and used in analytics (e.g. interest by region and product line).
Journey 5 â€“ Customer asks the expert (AI first, then human)
From the home screen (or the floating chat button), the customer types into the Ask AI box and picks a product category (or lets the AI suggest one).
Types the question and can attach a photo or drawing.
The AI assistant replies within seconds, using only Morgan-approved content, and shows the sources it used (e.g. a datasheet or manual).
The question and answer are saved as a thread in â€œMy Questionsâ€, so the user can come back to it later.
If the answer is not enough, the user clicks â€œTalk to an expertâ€.
If the designated product-line expert is online, a live chat opens in the same thread. If no one is online, the request is queued, the expert is notified by email, and the user is told when to expect a reply.
The expert replies; the user is notified by email and in the portal. The thread is marked â€œResolvedâ€ when done, and the user can rate the answer.
Journey 6 â€“ Morgan User (expert) handles customer chats
Morgan User signs in with their Morgan Microsoft account (single sign-on, no separate password) and sees only the chats for their product line(s).
Gets an instant notification (in-portal bell, browser notification and email) whenever a customer starts a new chat or updates an existing chat in their product line.
Opens the chat and sees the full thread (including the AI answer and attachments) next to a Customer panel: profile (name, company, country, region, language), all previous chats with this customer, and the activity log and journey (what they searched, which products they viewed, downloads and tools used, in date order).
Takes the live chat or replies in the thread; can reassign to another expert or product line.
Marks the thread resolved. Optionally flags the answer so admins can add it to the AI knowledge base.
Journey 7 â€“ Customer uses a tool
Logged-in user opens â€œPFP Certificate Finderâ€ or â€œCO2 Emission Reduction Estimationâ€.
Enters search filters or input values.
Gets results and can download files (e.g. certificates) where allowed.
The portal logs the tool use and downloads for analytics.
Journey 8 â€“ Admin manages the portal
Admin logs in to the admin panel.
Adds or edits Main Area pages, links, files and the access level (open / restricted).
Updates restricted tool data (e.g. uploads new PFP certificates, updates CO2 factors).
Creates alpha user IDs, assigns experts to product lines, and uploads documents for the AI knowledge base.
Views the analytics dashboard and exports reports.

How data is fetched and kept up to date
Source (preferred order): (1) the official site's CMS content API or a Morgan-provided export/feed â€“ the site's /media/ file paths suggest an Umbraco CMS, to be confirmed by Morgan; (2) if no API, the site's sitemap plus a polite crawler limited to morganthermalceramics.com, run with Morgan's permission; (3) manual upload in the admin panel for anything not on the site.
Sync job: runs nightly (Vercel Cron or Supabase Edge Function), per locale (e.g. /en-gb/, /de-de/). It reads each page or PDF, extracts title, summary, category, document type, language and last-modified date, and stores it in the Site Resource index in Supabase.
Change detection: only new or changed pages are processed (last-modified date / content hash); removed pages are hidden automatically and broken links are reported to the Super Admin.
AI knowledge: text from approved pages and PDFs is split into passages and stored in pgvector; every passage keeps its official URL so AI answers cite and link back to the official site.
Admin control: the Super Admin reviews newly found items, maps them to portal categories, hides anything not wanted, and can trigger â€œRe-sync nowâ€.
Click-through tracking: every â€œView on official siteâ€ link goes through a portal redirect (e.g. /go/{id}) that records a â€œproduct viewedâ€ event against the customer, then forwards to the official page.
How the index is stored and refreshed
The index lives in our own Supabase (PostgreSQL) database. We store only what the portal needs to list, search and answer â€“ not copies of the pages or PDFs. Files stay on the official site and are opened from there.

What the scheduler does on each run
Trigger: a schedule (Supabase pg_cron, nightly by default; frequency set by the Super Admin) or the â€œRe-sync nowâ€ button starts a run.

Compare: for each URL, check the siteâ€™s last-modified date (or a quick content hash) against site_pages. Unchanged pages are skipped â€“ this keeps runs fast.
Process new or changed pages: fetch the page or PDF, extract title, summary, image and text, suggest a category from the site menu, update site_pages, and rebuild that pageâ€™s passages and embeddings in site_chunks.
Handle removals: URLs no longer on the site are marked â€œremovedâ€ and disappear from the portal and from AI answers.
